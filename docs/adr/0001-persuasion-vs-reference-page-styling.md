# Persuasion pages get the dark hero band; reference and auth pages don't

Status: accepted

The Field design system's dark hero band (near-black background, blue radial glow) applies only to persuasion pages (homepage, pricing, features, compare) — reference pages (blog, help, supplier directory) and auth pages (signup, login, verify-card, check-email) stay on the warm paper background throughout, with only typography and token colors updated.

Considered giving every page the same dark hero for uniform visual consistency, but rejected it: a dark band at the top of a long-form compliance article, or at the exact moment someone is entering payment details, reads as either heavy or untrustworthy — the drama only earns its keep on pages actively trying to persuade. This means the site deliberately looks like two different registers depending on page type; that's intentional, not an inconsistency to "fix" later by making everything match.
